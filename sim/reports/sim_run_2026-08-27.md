## SECTION 2: CRITICAL FINDINGS

### CRITICAL-1: MP Economy is Never Sustainable

**Finding:** No race at any level band can sustain skill usage through natural MP Regen alone. Even Elf (INT growth = 1.0) at Level 300 recovers only 5.4 MP per 1000 CT against a skill cost of 20 MP.

**Evidence:**
| Race | Level | INT | Max MP | Regen/1kCT | Skill Cost | Casts Before Empty | Regen Covers? |
|------|-------|-----|--------|------------|------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Human | 99 | 33 | 86 | 2 | 20 | 4.3 | 6% |
| Elf | 99 | 101 | 222 | 4 | 20 | 11.1 | 12% |
| Elf | 300 | 302 | 624 | 9 | 20 | 31.2 | 27% |
| Orc | 99 | 9 | 38 | 2 | 20 | 1.9 | 6% |

**Level band where this matters most:** ALL levels. MP Regen never exceeds 27% of a single skill's cost per turn.

**Severity rationale:** This is BY DESIGN — the game intentionally uses MP as a scarce resource with post-battle recovery. However, the severity depends on how many skills per battle are expected. At 4.3 casts (Human L99), a typical 6-turn battle allows roughly 1-2 skill uses. If the design intends more frequent skill usage, external MP sources (Wand Arcane Flow, Recharge status, items) become mandatory rather than optional.

**Verdict:** Not a bug — verify this matches design intent for skill frequency.

### CRITICAL-3: Combat Slows 25% from Level 1 to Level 300

**Finding:** %HP damaged per 1000 CT declines from 24.6% (L1) to 18.4% (L300) in a balanced mirror match. This means fights take approximately 25% more turns at endgame.

**Evidence:**
| Level | %HP/1kCT | Turns to KO (2 atk/turn) |
|-------|----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | 24.6% | 8 |
| 99 | 25.1% | 6 |
| 150 | 22.5% | 7 |
| 300 | 18.4% | 9 |

**Cause:** HP grows at VIT×4 (linear) while damage formula produces diminishing returns via the harmonic-mean defense formula. After L99, items stop scaling but stats keep growing — VIT adds HP directly while STR's damage contribution faces the Atk²/(Atk+Def) curve.

**Severity rationale:** A 25% combat duration increase across 300 levels is gentle and likely acceptable. It does NOT represent a curve break — progression is smooth. However, if combined with Defense-stacking builds (Dwarf + Sword Breaker + heavy armor), endgame fights could become glacial for non-optimized attackers.

**Verdict:** Monitor but not critical. Consider whether this is intentional pacing.
