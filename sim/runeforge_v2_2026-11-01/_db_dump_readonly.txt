######## item_rarity_rules ['id', 'rule_name', 'rule_value']
1 | Purpose | Rarity grants a positive Bonus BP allowance. The generator may buy tiered numerical attributes and, more rarely, eligible Bonus Passives. Base item statistics, Item-Level scaling, native passive, rarity bonuses, and temporary effects remain separate layers.
2 | Rarity budgets | Common 0; Uncommon 100; Rare 150; Epic 200; Exalted 225; Legendary 250; Mythic 300; Transcendent 350. Normally 2H weapons receive double BP: Uncommon 200 / Rare 300 / Epic 400 / Exalted 450 / Legendary 500 / Mythic 600 / Transcendent 700. Unique items are authored.
3 | Bonus Passive limit | Standard equipment may spawn at most 1 Bonus Passive. A 2H weapon may spawn at most 2. The native passive does not count. Threshold bonuses, reused passives, elemental transformations, pattern conversions, negation/absorption, and other mechanical effects count as Bonus Passives. The Runeforge never adds Bonus Passives; it may remove one (USER DECISION 2026-11-01).
4 | Eligibility | Total BP is not sufficient by itself. Every Bonus Passive checks item type/family, minimum rarity, same-rarity passive gate, compatibility, and roll weight.
5 | Most common rolls | Numerical attributes are the normal result: base item stats, Primary Stats, and fixed Derived Stats. Bonus Passives are rare.
6 | Item-Level scaling | Native base item stats and the flat components of base-item-stat and Primary-Stat bonuses scale with Item Level. Percentage components, Derived bonuses, Threshold bonuses, and Bonus Passives remain fixed.
7 | Item scale | Scale(L) = 0.30 + 0.70 × ((L − 1) / 98)^0.55, for Item Level L ≥ 1. Level 99 equals 1.00.
8 | Percentage order | Aggregate percentages that affect the same base value, apply them only to that base value, round once, then add scaled flat bonuses. Percentage bonuses never multiply flat bonuses or bonuses from equipment, Doctrine, buffs, passives, or other sources.
9 | Base Primary Stat | Race growth earned through unit levels + permanent allocation. Equipment, Doctrine, buffs/debuffs, Race Perks, passives, and every other source are outside Base Primary Stat.
10 | Total Primary Stats | Base Primary Stat + all equipment bonuses + Doctrine + buffs/debuffs + other valid modifiers. Derived and threshold base values are calculated from Total Primary Stats.
11 | Derived bonuses | Derived bonuses directly modify only the named property. Each declares its operation: flat, percentage points, additive multiplier component, final multiplier, or incoming-effect multiplier. They do not scale with Item Level.
12 | Threshold bonuses | Direct Movement Range, Jump, Force, Stability, Discovery Radius, Interact Range, Item Range, and comparable breakpoint bypasses are Bonus Passives. They occupy the item’s Bonus Passive slot and do not scale with Item Level.
13 | Weight | Weight bonuses reduce the scaled base Weight by a percentage of scaled base Weight plus a scaled flat reduction. Raw Weight may become negative. The separate STR/negative-Weight formula resolves Effective Weight later.
14 | Budget completion | Choose predefined tiers only. Target total Bonus BP within ±5% of rarity BP. If no legal combination reaches the band, use the closest valid result below the minimum band. Never exceed the maximum band and never invent partial tiers. Applies to newly generated items only. Player-enchanted items may hold anywhere from 0 up to their rarity's normal budget (2H doubled); load checks must accept them (USER DECISION 2026-11-01, PLANNED — not built).
15 | Stacking | No rarity-specific equipment-wide stacking caps. Identical numerical percentages aggregate before one rounding step. Bonus Passives use their authored stacking rule. Existing global system caps remain.
16 | Safety rejection | Reject only unusable, undefined, recursive, technically unstable, infinite AP/RT/charge/trigger loops, duplicate save data, or map changes that can make battle completion impossible.
17 | Calculation Order - Primary Stats | Primary bonus = round(Base Primary Stat × aggregated percentage) + sum of scaled flat bonuses. Aggregate percentages first, apply once, round once. Flats added after. Equipment does not multiply other equipment.
18 | Calculation Order - Base Item Stats | Rarity stat bonus = round(Scaled Base Item Stat × aggregated percentage) + sum of scaled flat bonuses. Percentage uses scaled native item property BEFORE rarity bonuses.
19 | Calculation Order - Derived/Threshold | Derived from Total Primary Stats (after all Primary modifiers). Direct Derived/Threshold bonuses applied afterward. Derived = numerical (no passive slot). Threshold = passive slot.
20 | Overpowered Combinations | Overpowered combinations are acceptable. Only technically invalid, recursive, unstable, or battle-breaking combinations are rejected.
21 | Rarity Does Not Recalculate Base | Rarity does not pay for or recalculate the base item profile.
22 | Item Level Drop Assignment | Quest rewards: Item Level = Quest Level. Loot rewards: Item Level = Enemy Level.
23 | Affix Name Composition | Display Name = [Prefix] BaseName [of Suffix]. Prefix = affix name of the highest BP cost bonus on the item. Suffix = affix name of the second-highest BP cost bonus (if any). If only one bonus: Prefix only, no suffix. If zero bonuses (Common): base name only. Ties broken by: passive over numerical, then alphabetical family name. Enchanted items are renamed automatically by this same rule after every Runeforge change (USER DECISION 2026-11-01, E1).
24 | Affix Name Examples | Mighty Flameberge of Searing (STR T2 prefix + Burning Strike passive suffix). Honed Crossbow (Weapon Attack T1 prefix only, single bonus). Flameberge (Common, no bonuses). Titanic Warhammer of Brutality (STR T4 + Brutal passive). Arcane Wand of Channeling (INT T2 + Focus passive).
25 | Affix Source Table | item_affix_names table. source_type = 'numerical' or 'passive'. For numerical: source_family matches bonus_numerical_attributes.attribute_family, tier matches tier. For passive: source_family matches bonus_passives.passive_id, tier = 0 (passives have one name).
