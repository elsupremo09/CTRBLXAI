## 2. Exploits
- **No gold exploit.** Mercury, crystals, gems and sulfur cannot be sold, and enchanted items sell at their starting rarity. That closes every route tested.
- **Reset-to-0 control** (NOT the chosen rule): saves up to 28,516g per 2H item. The chosen exact rule stays safe as long as the Dev never resets the record.
- **Free Black Market (today's code):** it is a resource faucet, not a gold loop. Each object gives 12 crystals, about 13 gems and 6 × ceil(L/10) mercury for free. That is 3.8-4.4% of a lifetime's crystals per object.
- **Sulfur → gems:** a reroll can offer a set with more lines, so one reroll can add gems on dismantle: Exalted +2 (2H +6), Legendary +4 (2H +8), Mythic +5 (2H +10), Transcendent +5 (2H +11). This converts resources; it does not loop.
- **Fresh items forge cheaply** (credit 0, by design). Example: shop Common 1H L80 (1,032g) + forge fee 328g + 21 crystals = Epic L80 for 1,360g, against 6,192g for a shop Epic (22%). Crystals are the only gate.

## 4. Crystal shortage (T11) — choose nothing
- **Size:** need 460-685 against income 275-318 → short **142-410** crystals (31-60% of the need).
- **Battles to forge a full team of 45 items** (income 0.50-0.58 per battle):
  - to Uncommon: 135 crystals, 233-268 battles
  - to Rare: 405 crystals, 698-804 battles (1.3-1.5 lifetimes)
  - to Epic: 945 crystals, 1,628-1,877 battles (3.0-3.4 lifetimes); 546 crystals net of the 19 natural Epics, 940-1,084 battles
- **Gold through shop dismantling:**
  - cheapest is Rare Gloves/Feet: 295g per crystal at L10, 495g at L50, 740g at L99
  - the shelf caps supply at about 1.6 crystals per battle
  - closing the low gap costs 88-136k gold, the high gap 253-392k gold = **50-223% of lifetime income** → not viable as the main source for a free player
- **Single levers that close the realistic gap:**

| Lever | Value needed (low gap / middle / high gap) |
|---|---|
| Elite / boss crystals | ×1.96 (elite 6, boss 10) / ×2.86 (9 / 14) / ×3.75 (11 / 19) |
| Chest crystals at 100% | 1.3 / 2.5 / 3.7 per chest (the draft's 25% → 1 adds only 27) |
| Smaller steps | 2/4/8 + keep 1/2/4 → need 280-430; 2/3/6 → 230-355 (fits income); 1/2/4 → 140-215 |
| Shop dismantling as main source | +50-78 points of spending pressure (low gap) |

  - Best single fit: smaller steps of 2/3/6 (keep 1/2/4). It is the only lever that brings the whole 460-685 range inside the income. Raising elite and boss crystals ×2-2.9 closes the low-to-middle gap only. Not chosen.

## 5. Mercury (T12)
- **Sources:** battle drops 640, of which kills give 274 at 5 enemies (the draft assumed 8 enemies: 438). Dispatch 328 (draft guess 0.6 per battle). Dismantling 886-1,182 (48-55% of income).
- **Material wall:** 18.9 (75% dismantled) to 21.9 (100%) full L1→99 levellings, against a target of 15-20. The old 10-item scenario needs about 305-447 mercury.
- **Value that hits the target:**
  - at 75% dismantling, a kill chance of **5%** gives 17.5 (wall 20 needs ≤ 14%)
  - at 100% dismantling, even a 0% kill chance gives 19.1. With kills at 10%, dispatch must be ≤ 0.25 per battle to stay ≤ 20. **Kills 5% + dispatch 0.3 per battle gives 15.9-18.9 across 75-100% dismantling (inside 15-20)**
  - **Dispatch is the biggest unknown:** the DB rules allow 0.3-1.83 per battle (164-1,001 lifetime) at 50g per mission
- Daily events: +50 mercury per 10 events.

## 6. Thresholds, one unit (T17, information only, no cap)
- Movement +1, Jump +1, Force +1, Stability +1, Discovery +1: **+7 each** (1H + off-hand build, 7 items) or +6 with a 2H.
- Interact range (Surveyor) and Item range (Long-Throw): **+14** each (12 with a 2H). Weapon Range +1 and Arcane Reach: +1 each (weapon only).
- One passive per item, so these maxima exclude each other: at most 7 threshold passives per unit.
- Example: base AGI 81 at L99 with 7 Epic items (Movement +1 + AGI T1 on each) = Movement **12** (4 without gear).
- Seven T4 stat lines add +4 breakpoints at L99 (stat 81 → 332) and +8 at L300 (242 → 741).

## 9. Rule gaps and the assumption used
- **G1** Kill mercury "about 0.8 per battle" assumes 8 enemies; loot_progression 86 says Normal 5 → used 5 and reported 8 as well.
- **G2** Dispatch offer and refresh rate are not defined → used the draft's 0.6 and reported 0.3-1.83.
- **G3** Lifetime daily-event count is not defined → reported per 10 events.
- **G4** Black Market spawn rate is not defined; "six high-tier items" vs the draft's "6 Rare" → assumed 6 Rare, 1-5% spawn.
- **G5** The Fortune line "+X% Additive modifier" does not say what it adds to, and no general clamp at ×2.00 is stated (only "theoretical maximum"; the Idol row mentions a code clamp at 1.75) → tested readings A and B, both clamped.
- **G6** No roll weights for gem families, tiers or passives → assumed uniform, with 10% passives.
- **G7** Drops per battle 0.40 is a working estimate (5 enemies × 5% = 0.25 from kills) → kept 0.40.
- **G8** Epic items can only be levelled at Blacksmith L5+ (gf 13), so "enchant at L1, level at the needed discount" cannot be played; the real early-Epic route costs 29,028g (568g less, not an exploit).
- **G9** The forge fee is charged per forge: step-by-step forging pays 800g vs 400g in one forge (1H). "Route does not matter" holds for crystals, not for the fee.
- **G10** Elite crystals: "each completed elite" vs the elite reward chance of 75% × Fortune → assumed 100%, with no Fortune effect.
